WHY INSPECTION MATTERS

Know What Is Happening Below The Surface.

Much of a septic system is located underground, making it difficult to understand its condition through everyday use alone.

A professional inspection provides an opportunity to examine accessible components and identify visible conditions that may affect the system's operation.

Inspections can be particularly useful when buying or selling a property, investigating unusual system behavior, or planning maintenance work.

Identify Visible Conditions

Accessible components can be reviewed for signs of wear, damage, or concern.

Support Informed Decisions

Inspection findings can help homeowners understand potential next steps.

Plan Future Maintenance

Understanding system conditions can help guide future service and maintenance needs.

INSPECTION COVERAGE

A Closer Look At Your Septic System.

Depending on accessibility and the scope of the service, our inspection focuses on important components and visible conditions within the septic system.

01

Tank Condition

We assess accessible areas of the tank for visible signs of damage, deterioration, unusual conditions, or excessive accumulation.

02

Inlet & Outlet

Accessible inlet and outlet components can be reviewed for visible conditions that may affect wastewater movement.

03

Wastewater Flow

Where appropriate, observable flow conditions can help provide additional context about system operation.

04

Visible Concerns

We look for accessible signs of leakage, structural concerns, unusual odors, or other conditions that may require further evaluation.

Inspection scope depends on accessibility, property conditions, system design, and the purpose of the inspection. Some underground or inaccessible components may require additional evaluation.

WHEN TO INSPECT

An Inspection Can Be Useful At Key Moments.

Septic inspections are useful in several situations, especially when you need a clearer understanding of your system's current condition.

A professional assessment can provide useful information before making property decisions, beginning repairs, or addressing unexpected changes in system performance.

01
Buying A Property

An inspection can help you understand the accessible condition of an existing septic system.

02
Selling A Property

Inspection information can help document the condition of the septic system for relevant property discussions.

03
Unusual System Changes

Slow drains, odors, standing water, or other changes may justify professional evaluation.

04
Before Major Service

An inspection can provide useful context before repairs, maintenance, or other septic work is planned.
