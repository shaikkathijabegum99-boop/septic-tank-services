Signs Your Septic System Needs Attention

Recognizing early warning signs can help homeowners arrange service before a small issue becomes a larger septic problem.

WARNING SIGNS

Know When Your Septic System Needs Attention

Septic system problems can sometimes develop gradually. Paying attention to changes around your home can help identify potential issues before they become more serious or expensive to address.

1. Slow Draining Fixtures

Sinks, showers, bathtubs, or other fixtures that drain more slowly than usual may indicate a plumbing blockage or increased stress on the septic system. If multiple fixtures are affected, professional inspection may be appropriate.

2. Unpleasant Odors

Persistent sewage or wastewater odors around the tank, drainfield, or inside the home should not be ignored. Unusual odors can indicate that the system requires inspection or maintenance.

3. Standing Water Near The Drainfield

Pools of water or unusually wet areas around the drainfield may indicate that wastewater is not being absorbed properly. This can require professional evaluation to determine the cause.

4. Sewage Backups

Sewage backing up into sinks, showers, toilets, or other fixtures is a serious warning sign. Stop unnecessary water use and arrange professional septic service as soon as possible.

5. Unusually Green Grass

A patch of grass that is significantly greener or faster-growing than the surrounding area may indicate increased moisture or nutrient levels near the drainfield.

6. Gurgling Toilets Or Pipes

Gurgling sounds from toilets, sinks, or drains can sometimes occur when wastewater is having difficulty moving through the plumbing or septic system.

Warning Reminder

If you notice repeated backups, sewage odors, standing wastewater, or multiple drainage problems, arrange a professional inspection instead of waiting for the issue to worsen.
