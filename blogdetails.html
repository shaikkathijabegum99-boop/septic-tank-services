How To Maintain Your Septic System

Simple maintenance habits can help keep your septic system efficient, reliable, and ready for everyday use.

SEPTIC MAINTENANCE

Keep Your Septic System Working Properly

A septic system works every day to collect, treat, and manage household wastewater. Regular care can help reduce unnecessary strain and allow problems to be identified before they become major service issues.

1. Schedule Regular Pumping

Septic tanks should be professionally inspected and pumped according to household usage, tank size, and system conditions. Regular pumping helps prevent excessive solids from entering the drainfield.

2. Watch What Goes Down Your Drains

Avoid flushing wipes, paper towels, grease, chemicals, and other unsuitable materials. Toilets and drains should only receive materials designed for wastewater systems.

3. Protect Your Drainfield

Avoid parking vehicles or placing heavy structures over the drainfield. Excessive weight can damage pipes and compact the soil needed for proper wastewater treatment.

4. Fix Plumbing Problems Quickly

Leaking toilets and faucets can send unnecessary amounts of water into the septic system. Repairing leaks helps reduce hydraulic stress on the system.

5. Keep Maintenance Records

Keep a record of inspections, pumping dates, repairs, and other service visits. This makes it easier to understand your system's maintenance history.
